This page collects the resources behind this catalogue: the handbooks and courses that teach the foundations, the communities where practitioners help each other, the funders who back community connectivity, and the research that documents what works — especially in Global Majority contexts.

![Shelves of guides and reference books](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1521587760476-6c12a4b040da?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=70)

## Learning and guides

![Learning resources reaching the classroom](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1473649085228-583485e6e4d7?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=70)

**Foundations**

- [Community Network Readiness Assessment Handbook (Internet Society)](https://www.internetsociety.org/resources/doc/2022/community-network-readiness-assessment-handbook/) — assess whether your community is ready, then plan and build. Includes concept and techno-economic templates.
- [Community Network Readiness Assessment online course (Internet Society)](https://www.internetsociety.org/learning/community-network-readiness/) — a guided, worksheet-based version of the handbook.
- [Community Network Do-It-Yourself Toolkit (Internet Society)](https://www.internetsociety.org/resources/community-network-diy-toolkit/) — the whole journey in 10 steps, from community definition to sustainability.
- [APC Community Networks Learning Space](https://cnlearning.apc.org/) — topic-by-topic courses and readings on network management, sustainability, policy, and more.
- [Wireless Networking in the Developing World](https://wndw.net/) — the free, classic book on planning, building, and maintaining low-cost wireless networks.

**Deeper study**

- [Telecommunications Reclaimed: A hands-on guide to networking communities](https://netcommons.eu/telecommunications-reclaimed) — collective wisdom from the European community network movement, including policy and sustainability chapters.
- [Self-sustaining Financing Solutions for Community Connectivity (DC3, UN IGF)](https://comconnectivity.org/self-sustaining-financing-solutions-for-community-connectivity/) — the definitive study of financing models, with case studies from five continents. Also available via [APC](https://www.apc.org/en/pubs/self-sustaining-financing-solutions-community-connectivity-official-outcome-un-igf-dynamic).
- [Community-centred connectivity: A new paradigm (APC/Intermedia)](https://www.apc.org/en/blog/community-centred-connectivity-new-paradigm) — the policy case for community models.
- [Typology of community-centred connectivity initiatives (APC)](https://www.apc.org/en/pubs/typology-community-centred-connectivity-initiatives) — understanding the different organisational forms a CCCI can take.
- [Network Capacity as Common Pool Resource (Johnson et al., CSCW 2021)](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3449135) — how one community governs shared network capacity together.

## Communities and peer support

- [Dynamic Coalition on Community Connectivity (DC3)](https://comconnectivity.org/) — the UN IGF coalition where researchers, practitioners, and policymakers meet; publishes an annual outcome document.
- [APC Local Networks initiative (LocNet)](https://www.apc.org/en/projects/local-networks-locnet) — publications, peer exchanges, and subgrants for community networks, with a strong Global South focus.
- [Internet Society chapters](https://www.internetsociety.org/chapters/) — local chapters run trainings, grants programmes, and policy engagement in dozens of countries.
- [Community Networks Learning Space forum](https://cnlearning.apc.org/) — the community around APC's courses.
- Tool-specific forums (OpenWrt forum, LibreNMS community, OpenWISP users, Zabbix community) — most tools in this catalogue have active, welcoming support channels; links are on each tool's entry in the [catalogue](tools.html).

## Funding and finance

- [Internet Society grants and programmes](https://www.internetsociety.org/grants/) — community networks and digital inclusion funding.
- [APC subgrants](https://www.apc.org/en/projects/local-networks-locnet) — small grants for community network work, often via LocNet.
- [Universal Service Funds](https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Finance/Pages/USF.aspx) — public money earmarked for connecting the unserved; check eligibility in your country.
- [Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/) — transparent crowdfunding and money management built for open communities.
- [GSMA Mobile Money](https://www.gsma.com/mobilefordevelopment/mobile-money/) and [CGAP](https://www.cgap.org/) — research and practical guidance on inclusive finance and payments.

## Policy and advocacy

- [APC](https://www.apc.org/) — policy analysis and advocacy for community connectivity and digital rights.
- [Rhizomatica](https://rhizomatica.org/) — pioneer of community cellular policy reform in Latin America.
- [Internet Society policy programmes](https://www.internetsociety.org/policy/) — engagement on spectrum, access, and community networks.
- [ITU regulatory resources](https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regulatory-Market/Pages/National-Regulatory-Authorities.aspx) — directory of national regulators and regulatory market reports.

## Contribute

Know a tool this catalogue is missing, or spot something out of date? The database and this site are community-maintained:

1. Open the [database](https://docs.getgrist.com/8DBPFSG3ugAQ/FOSS4CCCIs-All-Tools-and-Platforms-v15-finalmergedtoolsplatformscsv-2026-06-1017-15) or the [site repository](https://github.com/FOSS4CCCIs/tools).
2. Propose the addition or correction (a pull request, or a message to the team).
3. When suggesting a tool, include: name, what it does, licence, owner/project, ease of use, and links to website, docs, and code.

Priorities for additions: FOSS alternatives to proprietary tools already listed, tools well-documented in Global South contexts, and platforms with strong localisation support.
